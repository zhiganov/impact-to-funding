# Periodic report template

**Template instructions:** select a proportionate format. Replace placeholders with supplied/inspected information or explicit unknowns. Default to a private draft; publication is separate. This is not an impact certificate.

- Person/project and actual role:
- Reporting period, preparation date and audience/disclosure basis:
- Earlier plan/theory and exact version reviewed, if available:

## What we intended

The useful change expected, activities/milestones, dependencies and assumptions. Separate delivery targets from intended outcomes.

## Contribution view, if relevant

Use the [contribution guidance](../references/contributions.md) for a personal or selected-portfolio report. Reference the relevant shared project theories/versions and actual role/period rather than copying each theory. Note changed fit, capacity conflicts, shared work and evidence limits; personal motivations are optional/private. Omit this section for project-only reporting when unnecessary.

## What happened and what did not

| Prior intention | Observed result/failure/unknown | Evidence ID, date and inspection limits | Outcome versus output; other contributors and alternatives | Implication for the next decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain abandoned/blocked intentions too | Do not infer missing results | Link only appropriately shareable evidence | No exclusive credit for joint work | Continue/change/support-existing/defer/stop |

## Evidence and actual review

Material claims, their support and limitations, actual reviewer/version/date if a review exists, contrary evidence and missing perspectives. No response is not approval. Identify claims superseded or corrected since the previous report. For each review, show its route (solicited or existing evaluation), review status, evidence-use limits and current claim support separately; a reviewer's withdrawal and a change in permission to use evidence are different events (see [claim review](../references/claims.md)).

After a challenge to a cited finding is acknowledged, show "challenged, unresolved" only to a permitted audience where that disclosure is safe. Qualify or omit dependent claims as appropriate; do not disclose the raiser or allegation, or even the challenge's existence where that could reveal protected information. An unresolved challenge does not by itself make a claim false; update the challenge status only when its actual outcome is recorded.

## Resources, costs and funding

Record expenditure information, funding received and remaining needs only where known and appropriate. Explain cost basis, existing support and actual funding-destination limits. Do not equate fundraising with social benefit or invent a return on donations.

## Harms, agency and burden

Who benefited or bore costs, whose view is missing, unintended effects, privacy/access concerns and whether reporting effort is justified. Do not publish identifying vulnerabilities or testimonies without permission.

## What changed and what comes next

Changed assumptions/theory; the next useful action or learning question; actual responsibility, resources and review conditions if agreed; explicit unknowns otherwise. A revised funding ask remains prospective.

## Disclosure review

Private material excluded; evidence links checked for disclosure; exact public text/destination and permission status if requested. No publication, outreach or fund movement is triggered by completing the report.
