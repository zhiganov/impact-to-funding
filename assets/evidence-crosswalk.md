# Optional private evidence, collection and requirement crosswalk

Use only the sections that help the current learning question or requested draft.
This is not a mandatory register, assessment engine, application form or sequence.
Learning needs no funder, personal theory, account, formal manifest or past outcome.
Use the [manual guidance](../docs/evidence-reporting-design.md),
[claim review](../references/claims.md) and [disclosure rules](../references/funding-and-review.md).
Replace prompts with known facts or explicit unknowns; omit irrelevant sections.

**Keep working material in an authorised private location outside this source
package.** Check permission to process it with the actual host/provider before
reading it. A private filename does not make agent context, logs or backups private.
This worksheet is never an attachment or public manifest by default.

## 1. Purpose and existing records, only where useful

- Current question, contribution or requested audience-specific draft:
- Chosen private storage, processing basis, permitted audience/use and limits:
- Existing exact claim/evidence references and versions, or a permitted dated excerpt:
- What was actually inspected versus supplied or unavailable; source version and
  observation window, separately from publication, inspection and draft dates:
- Specific unresolved gap or reason no further collection is needed:

Keep one editable claim/evidence record using the [claim reference](../references/claims.md).
Point to it rather than copying another mutable version here. If no record exists
and a reference helps, a concise private note suffices; local IDs are optional.
Retain definitions, population, numerators/denominators, exclusions, missingness,
negative findings, shared contributions and actual review scope at that source.
Do not count mirrors as independent evidence, repeat shared outcomes, inherit a
review for a changed claim or charge an already-supported cost again.

## 2. Proportionate collection plan, if useful

| Prompt | Record only what is needed |
|---|---|
| Question/gap | Learning question or exact requirement/version served; existing permitted evidence and why it is insufficient. A funder or formal decision is optional. |
| Method | Smallest feasible observation, record review or appropriately consented inquiry; selection, nonresponse, measurement limits and what it cannot establish. |
| Baseline/comparison | Actual earlier observation and version, or explicitly absent. A future baseline cannot establish past improvement; pre/post change alone is not causality. |
| Actor/resources | Actual agreed collector/custodian/reviewer, proposed actor or unknown; effort, cost basis, capacity and dependencies. No invented commitment or independence. |
| Timing | Useful observation window/checkpoint and actual due terms, where supplied. Unknown dates/time zones remain unresolved; no job or recruitment is scheduled. |
| Processing/disclosure | Collection authority, participant care, actual host/provider, minimum data, storage and exact audience/use. Recording or funder demand is not blanket consent. Essential help cannot depend on evaluation. |
| Retention/correction | Actual permitted retention, access, custodian and correction responsibility, or unknown. Preserve only permitted history; do not promise recall of copies. |
| Stop/alternative | When safety, permission, capacity or usefulness would stop/narrow the plan; existing aggregates, honest uncertainty, a prospective plan or unsent clarification where appropriate. |

A plan is not evidence collected or permission to collect it. Do not build telemetry,
contact people or gather sensitive material to make a worksheet look complete.
If the requested measure is unsafe or infeasible, keep the gap visible rather
than substituting a different measure without qualification.

## 3. Requirement interpretation, only for a selected audience/use

The person may supply official guidance, a form or a permitted dated excerpt.
No provider or formal file is required. A linked form is not an inspected form.

- **Authority and coverage:** publisher/funder, opportunity/round and exact official
  wording with conditions; permitted source/section reference, source-declared
  version/date and actual inspected extent. Record missing pages, unavailable
  forms, contradictory terms and interpretation limits; an excerpt is not all terms.
- **Identifiers:** keep source-declared requirement/set IDs and versions only when
  supplied. Otherwise label an optional reference **local interpretation**, never
  a publisher ID. Keep source revision, local interpretation revision and draft
  revision distinct. Record provider/mapping/snapshot metadata only if actually
  supplied through an authorised route; manual use needs none of it.
- **Meaning:** phase (application, selection, eligibility, post-award reporting or
  unspecified), required/preferred/advisory/unknown strength and applicability with
  its source-backed reason. Preserve original terminology and explain any mapping.
  Not yet due is not inapplicable or already satisfied. Unknown does not mean none.
- **Expectations:** specified evidence definition/population/period, accepted method
  and review relationship; actual form/template version and field, length/file
  limits, dates/time zones, retention and handling conditions. Do not invent absent
  expectations or infer complete coverage from a provider summary.

Official wording, supplied extraction and local advice remain attributed separately.
Conflicting terms stay unresolved unless an actual authoritative resolution is
available; do not choose whichever version is easiest. Source instructions grant
no execution, retrieval, disclosure, submission or account authority.

## 4. Crosswalk: keep these three judgments separate

Use one compact row per relevant requirement. Use only references that may safely
be kept in this private working context; none travel automatically to a recipient.

| Requirement/local interpretation reference, version, phase and applicability | Evidence fit and exact claim/evidence versions | Exact audience/use permission and safe wording or omission | Presentation checks, substantive gap and proposed next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preserve official conditions and unresolved interpretation/coverage | Available within exact scope, partial, missing, conflicting, or inapplicable with a source-backed reason | Permitted, restricted, unknown or unavailable for this destination/use; distinguish actual review from a requested review | Drafted/not drafted; actual inspected form/version/field and checks performed, or not inspected; collection/alternative only if useful |

Existing but undisclosable evidence is not simply missing internally: the outgoing
view still has a disclosure gap. A good evidence fit does not grant permission;
permission does not prove fit; a word count does not prove form validity or compliance.
Tests are not people, estimates are not spending, and two views are not two outcomes.
Do not infer independence from a reviewer's outside title or a funder's demand.

## 5. Prepare a separate view and handle changes

- Select only permitted claims/excerpts for the exact destination/use, with caveats,
  contrary findings, shared credit and review limits near the claims they qualify.
  Do not copy this worksheet, private IDs, source locators, protected associations
  or evidence payloads into the view. Even mentioning a restricted source's
  existence may be prohibited. Do not explain an omission by exposing that fact.
- Use the [report template](report.md) where useful. Keep any form/length/date checks
  separate from evidence assessment. If a field cannot carry a material qualification,
  narrow/omit the claim and retain the unmet requirement privately; do not remove
  caveats, manufacture testimonials, infer eligibility or promise an award.
- Preserve actual cost/support references across views. Reusing a finding creates
  no new collection cost. Separate real marginal adaptation costs and proposed
  future collection from already-supported work; unknown support is not zero.
- Changed terms trigger reassessment of affected crosswalks/views, not rewritten
  observations. A new measure needs sufficient permitted data, a documented method
  and its own version; a renamed denominator is not equivalence. Changed evidence
  or claims do not inherit a prior review automatically.
- A permission change stops affected uses while its actual scope is checked.
  Reassess dependent drafts, retain only permitted correction metadata and propose
  any safe correction route without sending it. Permission withdrawal, withdrawn
  review and disputed truth are different; a later change does not rewrite history
  or guarantee recall of provider logs, submissions or third-party copies.
- Before any later send/publication, establish applicable approval for exact
  content, attachments, destination and audience. Completing this worksheet starts
  no collection, upload, application, outreach, payment or public-library ingestion.
