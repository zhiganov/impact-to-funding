# Assessment packet template

**Template instructions:** use only the sections needed for the requested decision. Replace placeholders with sourced information or explicit unknowns; do not leave these instructions in the output. Default to a private draft. This template is not a verified-impact certificate.

## Profile and decision

- Person/chosen identity and project(s):
- Actual role and other contributors:
- Stage and current decision:
- Reporting/assessment period and preparation date:
- Intended beneficiaries and who defines success:
- Disclosure status and permitted audience:

### Plain-language summary

What is being attempted, the person's actual contribution, what is supported, what is uncertain, and the decision this assessment helps make. Keep aspirations separate from observed results.

## Theory of change

- Problem, local/contextual relevance and evidence:
- Importance; specific neglected gap after existing provision; realistic fixability:
- Alternatives, including supporting existing work or doing nothing new:

| Activities | Outputs | Expected/observed outcomes | Intended impact | Mechanism and material assumptions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Describe actual/proposed activity separately | Tangible delivery, not presumed benefit | Label observation versus expectation | Long-term hypothesis unless demonstrated | Why the link should hold; how it could fail |

- Control, partners, capacity and dependencies; unconfirmed commitments:
- Other contributors and plausible counterfactual:
- Harms, burden, exclusion, agency and missing perspectives:
- Evidence that would change the theory or justify stopping:

## Claims and evidence

Use separate rows for materially different claims; an evidence register can remain private.

| Claim ID and exact statement | Type, scope and period | Evidence IDs; dates and actual access | Support and reasoning | Alternatives, limitations and other contributors | Actual review provenance; version/date | Disclosure/correction status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Observation/proposal/etc. | E1; distinguish supplied versus inspected | No automatic certification | Include conflicting evidence and missingness | None if no actual review | Private/public candidate; permission basis |

### Source register

For each evidence ID: source/owner, what was supplied or inspected, relevant observation/publication/retrieval dates, scope/limitations and permitted access/disclosure. Do not expose sensitive source URLs in a public candidate.

## Proportionate evaluation plan

| Decision/question | Outcome or concern | Baseline and useful evidence | Method and limitations | Actual owner/capacity; consent, access and burden | Checkpoint and stopping condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Why learn this now? | Not only activity volume | Existing records first | Distinguish change from causal proof | Unknown if not agreed | No invented dates or execution approval |
