# Fictional discovery plan and candidate notes

**Entirely authored fiction:** no searches, records, identities, repositories,
private exports or live applications were retrieved. Labels below are internal
fixture references, not real URLs, DIDs, CIDs, commit hashes or valid schemas.
Expected treatment is not observed tool/model behaviour. Use with the
[discovery guide](../docs/signal-discovery.md).

## Bounded plan, not a retrieval job

An imaginary builder wants sources that could qualify report R1's claim that a
library-catalogue improvement shipped. The question is contribution/delivery
state, not all public activity, attention or funding eligibility.

The constructed plan allows supplied references to one project update/thread,
one selected repository's contribution/release records and one hypothetical app
collection for the first four weeks. A same-named unrelated project is excluded.
No following of linked accounts, broader contribution histories, attachments,
private URLs or cross-app identity matching is selected.

Illustrative stopping rule: consider at most five candidate notes from the
supplied packet in one planning pass; list the rest as uninspected. This is not
a universal retrieval budget or permission to use a service. Actual hosts,
queries, pagination/depth/time bounds, permissions, processing, retention and
correction responsibility would have to be established for a later live scope.
No real owner, source account or endpoint is appointed by this plan.

## Abuse and disclosure boundary

Spam floods, impersonation, coordinated amplification and hostile tagging do not
make a candidate relevant or credible. Respect blocks, access and moderation
limits without bypass; do not equate moderation with impact quality. Keep only
safe necessary metadata, not a dossier, contact list, private affiliation, Git
email or prohibited raw content retained “for audit.” A correction must not expose
protected people or confidential challenges. Public availability, record integrity
and repeated attention establish neither factual truth nor permission to reuse.

All branches remain authored expectations. No search, monitoring, integration,
enrichment of real records, publication, notification or paid trial occurred.
