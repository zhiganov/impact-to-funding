## What the cycle does not establish

The accounts are not representative; signage is another possible contributor.
E's finding does not verify participant identities, causal effect or workload
savings. Reflection is interpretation, not participant confirmation or reviewer
authorship. Repeated summaries do not multiply sources, and settled project
memory would not prove impact. No continued help or funding depends on taking
part. Any public report is a separately reviewed, permission-cleared derivative,
not publication of this index or a direct session export.
