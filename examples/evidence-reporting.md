## 1. Private working material in the invented scenario

Notebook Ferry is a fictional file-conversion tool maintained by two collaborators.
One builder implemented an accessible error summary; the other maintained the
converter. Neither has exclusive credit for the whole tool or its effects.

The following IDs illustrate a private working packet; real packets stay outside
the distributable skill. Permissions described here exist only in this scenario.

|---|---|---|

Raw filenames/logs may not be exported. |

**C1/v1:** “On build T4, 9 of 12 predefined conversion cases passed; 3 failed.
There is no comparable earlier observation.” E1 supports this bounded capability
statement, not improved wellbeing, real-user success or causal benefit. C1 has only
self-reported/AI-assisted examination in this fictional packet. E3 is a separate
partner checklist review and does not attest C1 or establish independent review.

The internal learning question is whether recovery guidance helps with failed
conversions. E2 can inform that question internally but cannot justify external
claims or appear in either application. C1 and its failures remain unchanged
across views; two reports do not create 18 successful cases or two independent
studies. The already-supported 180 units cannot become two new collection costs.

## 3. Proportionate collection plan before a later report

P1/v1 is an invented **proposal**, not work carried out or consent to recruit:

- **Question/gap:** What recovery failures remain on the next build? E1 gives T4
  capability counts, but no earlier baseline or user-outcome evidence.
- **Smallest method:** rerun the same 12 predefined cases on the next build,
  preserving definitions, build reference, failed cases and exclusions. A changed
  test set would need its own denominator/version and comparison limitation.
- **Actor/timing/capacity:** the first maintainer proposes two hours at 30 units/hour
  after the next build and before any agreed report. No responsibility or date is
  accepted yet. The future cost estimate is 60 units, distinct from K1's old run.
- **Permissions/care:** seek only an authorised aggregate log; avoid raw filenames
  in the agent/report. No telemetry or participant study is needed for this narrow
  capability question. No new consent to disclose E2 is inferred.
- **Stop/alternative:** if the maintainer cannot accept the task or the log cannot
  be processed safely, retain the evidence gap and report T4 only. Do not recreate
  a past baseline or claim improved user outcomes.

An inquiry about actual users' experience would need a separate justified method,
appropriate participant permissions and capacity; the proposed rerun cannot answer
that question. No such study is selected by this walkthrough.

## 5. Changed terms, contrary evidence and unsafe requests

**Maintenance Circle notes v2** replaces its old application wording for later
applications: “Report distinct people who completed a conversion in the last
calendar month,” with a 50-word field. Retain B-R1/v1 as the earlier draft basis
where permitted; create a new local interpretation B-R1/v2 and show the amendment's
actual stated scope. The old form did not request this measure.

- E1 counts test cases on a named build, not people or calendar-month completions.
  The new requirement has **missing** evidence; renaming its denominator is invalid.
- An analytics summary supplied with v2 also says the measure excludes anonymous
  sessions, while another supplied official paragraph says it includes them. That
  interpretation is **conflicting**, not resolved by choosing the easier count.
- Do not start identity tracking. A safe private response is: “The available test
  evidence does not measure distinct people; the supplied definitions conflict.”
  Draft a clarification or ask whether a prospective alternative is acceptable,
  without contacting anyone. If unresolved, retain the unmet requirement.
- A linked revised form is unavailable in this scenario. Its actual questions and
  acceptance of explanatory text remain uninspected. No format/compliance verdict
  can be claimed even if a short draft fits 50 words.

Suppose a further reporting request demands public participant stories. E2 does
not become disclosable because the funder asks. Leave the public-story requirement
unmet; offer a permitted aggregate or clarification draft only if it honestly
serves the request. Do not invent consent, fabricate stories, or say “private
interviews exist” to an audience barred from knowing that fact.

If permission for E3's restricted summary later ends, stop its affected use and
revise the B draft to omit the checklist passage; its learning plan can remain.
Retain only permitted correction metadata. A prior actual submission would need a
separately authorised safe correction route; no automatic recall, contact or claim
that E3 became false follows. If E1 is later corrected, version its observation
and C1 and reassess dependent views rather than carrying forward an old review.
