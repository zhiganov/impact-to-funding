# Synthetic input — five-project contribution portfolio

**Entirely fictional.** All people, projects, periods, sources and amounts below
are invented for this example, not adapted from private pilot records. The input
is separate from the [authored illustrative answer](portfolio-assessment.md).

## Request from Person A

Help me review my direction and next commitments across five projects. I want to
strengthen practical cooperation, but I am also exploring ecological restoration;
I do not know whether these should be one personal pathway. Keep the result
private. Do not contact anyone, browse for missing records or submit funding asks.

I have six hours available in fictional period B. Lantern is already allocated
four of those hours. Bridge would take another four hours if I agree; the other
future allocations are unknown. Does my portfolio justify saying that I created
five successful community projects? Could both Bridge and Compass fund the draft
they each want to use? D is not part of Lantern, so can I call D's check an
independent review?
