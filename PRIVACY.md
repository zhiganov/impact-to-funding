# Privacy and data handling

The skill is a set of instructions, references, templates and fictional examples. It does not bundle an application server, analytics, payment service or automatic uploader. **This does not make your agent session local-only, confidential or free of retention.**

## Where your information can go

| Surface | What to establish before use |
|---|---|
| Your chosen agent and model | Material pasted into chat or read through tools can enter model context and reach the provider. Check that host/provider's permissions, retention, training and sharing settings; do not infer them from this repository. |
| Agent history and local outputs | Sessions, tool logs, backups and generated files may retain material. Choose appropriate storage, access and retention, not just an output filename. |
| Optional private evidence/crosswalk worksheet | Keep working records outside this source package and check the actual host/provider processing basis first. Evidence fit, exact destination/use permission and presentation checks are independent. Do not attach the worksheet or export restricted IDs, locators, payloads or protected associations; even acknowledging a source's existence may disclose too much. |
| Optional local support-profile tools | They read only explicitly supplied local files and create new local candidate bundles on request. They make no network/publication calls. Diagnostic redaction and restrictive output permissions are not permission to process inputs or publish outputs; inspect the candidate and its loss/provenance notes for the exact destination. |
| Evidence retrieval and other tools | Opening a source can reveal request information or send data to an external service. Permission to inspect a file is not permission to upload it to another provider. Keep signed/private links and access credentials out of public outputs. |
| GitHub and distribution | Public source, issues, PRs, branches and release archives are not a private workspace. Do not attach raw project packets or real beneficiary material to a bug report. |
| Fundraising destinations and future integrations | Their ownership, privacy and eligibility requirements are separate. Local support-profile preparation and the optional public-library MCP do not supply a hosted funding provider, application submission, account setup, public-report publisher or payment integration. |

There is no in-product consent mechanism, access-control system or audit service here. Safety instructions are not a sandbox or enforcement guarantee. The host's actual tools and permissions govern execution.

## Use the minimum necessary material

- Establish that you may process the material for this purpose with the selected host/provider. Consent to recording or internal learning does not automatically allow fundraising, publication or transfer to another service.
- Prefer permitted summaries, aggregates and relevant excerpts over full recordings, transcripts, contact lists or sensitive case files.
- Do not provide passwords, tokens, payment credentials, precise vulnerability locations, health details or beneficiary identities simply to make an assessment look rigorous.
- Keep private working evidence outside the distributable checkout. `.gitignore` reduces accidental inclusion; it does not prevent the agent reading a file or a person force-adding it.
- A public source can be relevant evidence without granting permission to portray someone as endorsing your work.

## Private analysis is not a public profile

The working packet can contain uncertainties, restricted evidence and candid interpretations. A public candidate must be curated separately: disclose only permitted claims and supporting material, with relevant dates, scope and limitations.

Removing a name or calling a story synthetic does not guarantee anonymity. A lightly modified real case can remain identifiable. Use genuinely fictional examples for public reproduction when disclosure permission is absent, and do not invent a review or result while doing so.

Affected people should not have to publish their story, reveal vulnerabilities or participate in evaluation to receive essential help. Local or restricted evidence can be meaningful without being publicly accessible; its access and review limits should be stated honestly.

## Corrections, retention and reporting

Agree appropriate retention and access with the people responsible for real project records. Keep corrections and supersession clear where claims have already been used. Deleting local files or a public record cannot guarantee that provider logs, backups or third-party copies disappear.

For [evidence/crosswalk work](assets/evidence-crosswalk.md), changed terms trigger
reassessment of the affected view without rewriting observations or inheriting
reviews. A permission change stops affected uses while its scope is checked.
Distinguish disclosure withdrawal, review withdrawal and disputed truth; retain
only permitted correction metadata and authorise any recipient contact separately.
Working records never enter the public reference library or its MCP automatically.

If you find an accidental disclosure, do not quote or re-upload the sensitive material in a public issue. Stop further sharing and identify the affected surface and owner. Use only a verified, appropriate confidential reporting route; none is promised by this repository. If no such route is established, a public report should contain only a non-sensitive description asking how to report privately—not the evidence itself.
