# P002 — Keep distinct outcomes distinct

**Candidate learning/design pattern, record revision 1, prepared 2026-11-08.**
Case-informed ItF interpretation, not an evaluated programme package, success
rating or claim that the original authors used this pattern name.

## Context and recurring problem

A contribution plausibly serves several purposes, or a theory links one change
to another. An encouraging result, certification or attractive headline is used
to imply that all the intended benefits occurred. An equally misleading response
is to dismiss a meaningful result because a different benefit was not demonstrated.

## Forces

- Useful progress needs to be recognisable, while source findings often differ
  by outcome, population, period and method.
- Easily counted delivery or uptake can displace harder questions about benefit.
- Funding and communication incentives favour one clean story; measurement also
  costs time and can place burdens on people doing or experiencing the work.

## Adaptable response

Name the distinct intended changes and their proposed relationships. Keep clear
what was delivered, what changed, for whom and over which period, and which
further links remain assumptions. Let mixed evidence inform adaptation rather
than filling causal gaps with optimism or declaring the whole contribution worthless.

Measure or investigate only what the purpose and stakes justify, using existing
appropriate evidence first. Keep uncertainty when a link cannot proportionately
be examined. A useful measured outcome can remain valuable in its own right;
housing stability, for example, need not be reduced to a tool for clinical improvement.

## Case basis and counterweight

All links refer to case-record revision 1. These contrasting findings motivate
the learning pattern; no study randomised use of P002 itself:

| Case | What must remain distinct |
|---|---|
| [C004 — Housing First](../cases/at-home-chez-soi.md) | Substantially more stable housing, smaller added quality-of-life/functioning gains, similar clinical-symptom changes between groups, and heterogeneous cost offsets. A housing benefit is not automatic clinical recovery or a universal cash saving. |
| [C005 — Mali CLTS](../cases/mali-community-led-sanitation.md) | Increased latrine access and incomplete measured behaviour change despite government certification, no detected primary diarrhoea reduction, and positive but qualified secondary linear-growth findings. Gains do not settle coercion or dignity concerns. |
| [C001 — Ahmedabad](../cases/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md) | Implemented response measures, a post-plan mortality association, modelled avoided mortality and nonsignificant/sensitivity results. None is a count of identified people causally saved by the plan. |
| [C003 — TaRL](../cases/teaching-at-the-right-level.md) | Materials distribution, actual teaching practice, reading and maths effects, and tested delivery packages. Positive and null variants do not justify one result for every implementation or educational purpose. |
| [C002 — Making All Voices Count](../cases/making-all-voices-count.md) | A channel's existence, participation, service responsiveness and durable accountability. Inspected summaries provide reported learning, not verified effects on all those outcomes. |

Null findings are not proofs of equivalence or no possible effect. Secondary,
exploratory, observational and randomised results retain their different meanings.
The cases are not commensurate inputs to an impact score or a domain ranking.

## Consequences, failure conditions and alternatives

This approach can support a narrower honest claim, a revised pathway or recognition
that the work serves a different purpose than first assumed. It must not become
an exhaustive evaluation form, a demand for sensitive data, a condition for essential
help or a way to dismiss outcomes people value because they are hard to quantify.
When an assessment is unnecessary, a plain explanation with limits may be enough.

## Language connections and framework questions

**Complements [P001 revision 1](response-behind-the-channel.md):** examining the
response behind information helps identify missing links, while distinct outcome
claims prevent that plausible response from being described as proven benefit.
**Potential tension:** a burdensome indicator/reporting regime can weaken actual
delivery. Proportionate learning, not maximal measurement, is the aim.

Optional [L001 revision 1](../lenses.md#l001--doughnut-economics) asks whether visible
benefits conceal unmet social needs, unequal burdens or ecological effects.
[L002 revision 1](../lenses.md#l002--planetary-boundaries) asks which environmental
pressures were actually examined rather than inferred from a positive label.
Neither lens supplies an unmeasured outcome, local consent or framework conformance.
