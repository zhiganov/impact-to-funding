# P001 — Put usable response capacity behind information

**Candidate pattern, record revision 1, prepared 2026-11-08.** Case-informed ItF
interpretation, not a validated intervention, universally necessary component or
claim that the source programmes used this name.

## Context and recurring problem

A contribution supplies warnings, feedback or assessments with an intended effect
beyond transmitting information. People or institutions must do something different,
but the means, authority, relationships or time to respond may be missing.

## Forces

- A new channel, dashboard or assessment is visible and easier to deliver/count
  than changes in everyday practice or institutional response.
- Information may reach someone without the resources, power, safety or incentives
  to act. Adding messages can increase frontline burden rather than resolve it.
- Established relationships can make action possible while also filtering whose
  needs count, excluding people or consolidating gatekeeper power.

## Adaptable response

While developing the contribution, examine how information could become usable
action: who can respond, what they can actually do, whose agreement is needed,
what time/resources and relationships it requires, and how the people affected
can influence or contest the response. Work through an existing useful arrangement
where possible, rather than assuming a new platform creates capacity.

Keep a proposed role separate from an accepted responsibility. A warning or
feedback channel should not imply that support is available when it is not.
This is a question about the contribution's mechanism, not permission to contact
people, collect more data, promise services or assign work.

## Case basis and counterweight

All links below refer to case-record revision 1. They support examination of the
hypothesis, not a common component-specific causal estimate:

| Case | What informs the candidate | What is not established |
|---|---|---|
| [C001 — Ahmedabad](../cases/ahmedabad-heat-action-plan.md) | Published model and implementation account link warnings with responsibilities, outreach and health capacity; coordination was revised after an exercise. | The mortality evaluation is observational and cannot isolate the response arrangement's causal effect. |
| [C002 — Making All Voices Count](../cases/making-all-voices-count.md) | Publisher-reported learning stresses offline relationships and actors able to respond to citizen feedback. | Full reports were not inspected; this is not a verified sufficient recipe for accountability or protection from reprisals. |
| [C003 — TaRL](../cases/teaching-at-the-right-level.md) | Contrasting delivery packages and process evidence connect assessment with protected teaching practice and practical support. | No factorial test isolates time, mentoring or assessment; some materials-only results were positive, so a blanket "information alone never helps" rule is wrong. |

These are different mechanisms and evidence types. Municipal response to heat,
political accountability and classroom instruction are not interchangeable because
all involve information. Transfer requires explaining the locally relevant actors,
relationships and constraints rather than copying the package or claiming recurrence
proves effectiveness.

## Consequences, failure conditions and alternatives

The approach may reveal that no credible response is presently available. Narrow
the promise or reconsider the contribution rather than fabricating an owner or
shifting responsibility to people without options. More process, central control,
surveillance or unpaid coordination can cause harm. Some information has legitimate
value in its own right without any promised downstream response; do not impose
institutional arrangements on that different purpose.

## Language connections and framework questions

**Complements [P002 revision 1](keep-outcomes-distinct.md):** a plausible response
arrangement does not establish that the intended benefit occurred. Conversely,
measuring a channel's use does not establish an adequate response arrangement.
**Potential tension:** measurement/reporting demands added under P002 can consume
the response capacity this pattern is trying to make real; use proportionate evidence.
