**Proposed title:** Consent decisions: keep withdrawn objections on record, and say who may propose

---

## Context

Impact to Funding is checking whether a guild could run its decisions through Community Input ([ItF #102](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/102), [My Community #140](https://github.com/Citizen-Infra/my-community/issues/140)). Reading the dashboard code, the consent model fits most of a fictional guild's rule change: a window, objections with reasons, amended re-posts and superseded versions. Two things couldn't be checked from the dashboard, because they live in Community Admin.

## 1. Withdrawn objections

A member can change their answer until the window closes. If someone raises an objection and later withdraws it, does Community Admin keep the earlier objection anywhere?

A guild may want a withdrawn objection kept on record, with its reason, so later readers can see the concern was raised and resolved. Identities could stay visible to organizers only, as they are now.

**Suggested outcome:** decide whether the objection history is kept. If it is, show withdrawn objections on the ratified decision, marked as withdrawn.

## 2. Who may propose

Who can create a consent decision: organizers only, or any member? Is it set per community?

A guild whose rules let any member propose a change needs that, or a documented way for a member to ask an organizer to post it.

**Suggested outcome:** document the current rule, and say whether it can be set per community.

## Out of scope

Majority voting. Communities whose rules need a majority can read the agree count; this asks for no new decision rule.
