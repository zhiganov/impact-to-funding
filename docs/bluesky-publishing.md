# Optional Bluesky publication and corrections

**Status:** original manual planning guide with
[authored fictional cases](../examples/bluesky-publishing.md). No account/client,
post, reply, quote, upload, notification, live trial or working publisher has
been established. This document does not authorise any of those actions.

The smallest route is an ordinary draft reviewed and posted through an existing
appropriate client, if the person later selects that action. Compare an already
authorised tool before proposing automation; no dedicated ItF account, custom
Lexicon, new service or Groups deployment is required. Discovery and enrichment
do not grant publication permission. Nor does this guide select recruitment,
a Moral Ambition introduction, funder contact or promotion.

Reuse [privacy](../PRIVACY.md), [claim review](../references/claims.md), the
[report](../assets/report.md) and the public-derivative/correction principles in
the [completed public-export design](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/17#issuecomment-6665579580).
That design's proposed application/protocol controls are not available Bluesky
features by inference. A public post is not a private workspace or impact badge.

## 1. Establish authority and prepare the public derivative

Identify who may speak for this person/project, the actual source account and
its authority, intended destination/audience and the exact public artifact/version.
Account control, signature, membership and access to evidence do not establish
authority to speak for others. A handle alone is not a permanent identity;
record the actual account DID/observed handle where known without exposing
unnecessary private identity or association information.

Keep the private working packet separate. Curate only permitted bounded claims,
actual roles/shared credit, reporting period and necessary limitations. A review
stays bound to its precise target; do not turn a code review, endorsement or AI
summary into independent impact verification. Likes, clicks and posts establish
neither benefit nor funding. If the short format cannot carry material caveats,
use a neutral description and approved public report link, not a stronger headline.
Personal theories remain optional; funding.json v1.1.0 financial fields are
unchanged and no funding destination is invented or activated.

Before real publication, establish the actual update owner, controlled report/
post/preview destinations, safe correction route and a proportionate review or
expiry condition. No updater, reporting service or response SLA is supplied here.
If there is no continuing updater, use an honestly dated snapshot, not a claim
that the linked report is always current.

## 2. Preview the entire outgoing action

Prepare this in ordinary Markdown first; do not send it. Replace unknowns only
with established facts, not a presumed account, recipient or permission.

```text
Action: standalone post | link post | reply | quote | correction/replacement |
  deletion, with the exact controlled target for any existing record
Speaker/account: actual authorised account and observed identity, or unknown
Destination/audience: actual client/service and public surface; relevant limits
Exact outgoing text, including dates, qualifications and visible link labels:
Canonical public report URL and exact approved version; current-status limits:
Link/mention facets and tags: actual destinations/accounts, not just rendered text
Reply: root and immediate-parent references, separately; none if standalone
Quote: exact target/version and surrounding context; none if not quoting
Assets: exact images/files and alt text/captions/metadata, or none
Link card: exact title/description/thumbnail/URL and how it is supplied, or none
Expected outward effects: notifications, target/reference exposure, uploads,
  preview fetches or other service processing, with actual capability unknowns
Approval: who authorised this exact action/version/account/destination and when;
  not approved until actually recorded
```

Review all outward surfaces: text, actual links/facets, tags, image contents and
metadata, filenames, alt text, cards, redirects/URL parameters and retained logs.
Exclude secrets, signed/private URLs, private source IDs, rosters, personal
theories, protected associations, sensitive cases and restricted reviewer details.
Public access does not grant quotation/republication rights or endorsement.

Preparing a plain Markdown preview does not require fetching links or uploading
assets. The chosen agent/provider still processes supplied text under its own
terms. In a real client, preparing a card or attaching an image may itself send
material to services before posting.
Establish that processing basis first; do not upload private evidence to find
out what the preview looks like. If safe metadata cannot be controlled, omit
the card/asset or use another appropriate route; never assume a clean visible
caption means the whole outgoing object is safe.

Obtain action-specific approval before sending. A reply, quote, mention or tag
can expose a relationship or notify people and must not be added as incidental
metadata. Do not auto-reply to discovered mentions, tag reviewers/funders, switch
accounts or change targets after approval. Material changes require a revised
preview and approval. A request to draft, share a report somewhere else or publish
source code is not approval to publish a Bluesky post.

## 3. Send only if selected; distinguish attempts from confirmed records

Check the chosen client's actual capability, account context, format limits,
preview behaviour and access/moderation/rate constraints before real use. Do not
assume an in-place edit, a particular audience control, tool/API parity or working
authentication. Do not request credentials, create an account or post a test to
prove access under this guide. If the necessary capability is unavailable, retain
the draft or use an actually supported, authorised manual route—not an invented
publisher, alternate identity or access bypass.

For any later selected send, bind the attempt to the approved preview. Preserve
the provider response/receipt safely, including actual account, record URI/CID,
readable destination and outcome where supplied. Keep credentials and unnecessary
private diagnostics out of logs. With authorised inspection, verify the actual
record/account/content/embeds and known visibility limits before calling it
published as approved. A CID identifies content, not its truth or social value.

An uncertain timeout or missing local receipt is **not permission to resend**.
Use the retained response and a bounded authorised check of that exact account/
record/intended content; do not enumerate history or post duplicates to test it.
Identical text alone does not identify the intended send. A missing read result
can have access/indexing/availability limits and does not prove the send failed.
If reconciliation remains inconclusive, stop the sending route and report the
uncertainty. Do not silently reset its attempt budget or infer approval for a
replacement. No universal idempotency or retry mechanism is promised here.

## 4. Correct controlled copies without claiming global recall

Reuse the [canonical correction rules](../references/claims.md#updates-reviews-and-disputes)
and [public propagation design](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/18#issuecomment-6666459669).
Keep source lifecycle, evidence-use/access, reviewer withdrawal, current claim
support and public-copy status separate. A reviewer withdrawal alone does not
make a claim false; a restriction on public quotation can leave private assessment
permitted. An unavailable source is not proof of deletion.

Stop queued/further unsafe sharing. The actual owner prepares any correction to
the controlled report, post and preview/asset sources within their authority.
A correction reply, replacement post or deletion is a new outward action: preview
its exact text/target/account/notification effects and obtain the applicable
action-specific approval, including any already-established incident authority.
Do not assume an old posting approval covers removal or a new post. Preserve
material qualifications and version relations; never reproduce restricted text
or private allegations in a correction notice, quote or screenshot.

Keep only permitted history. Record propagation as requested, acknowledged,
applied or unconfirmed for each controlled destination, not one “fixed everywhere”
flag. Updating a report does not prove a card, retained post, export or mirror
changed. Deletion cannot guarantee recall of caches, search results, provider
logs or third-party copies. Purge requests, recipient contact and monitoring
are separate actions, not automatically launched correction steps.

Respect blocks, labels, anti-spam/rate limits and the selected audience without
bypass, impersonation or repeated unsolicited contact. Factual challenges and
abuse are different questions; moderation labels are not impact findings. A safe
public correction must not reveal a reporter, protected identity or allegation,
or even a confidential challenge's existence where that would disclose them.
No moderation or appeals service is established by this guide.

## Documentation basis and limits

Official [post/reply/facet/embed examples](https://atproto.com/blog/create-post)
were consulted through Context7 on 2026-11-08; the
[current post record schema](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/packages/bsky/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/post.json)
was inspected for the companion [discovery guide](signal-discovery.md).
These explain why text, references and embeds need distinct review, not a pinned
client contract, current upload limit, available edit operation, enforced privacy
setting or tested send/delete behaviour. No SDK code or third-party template is
copied here. The [Standard.site note](standard-site-publishing.md) remains optional
document-publication context, not a working publisher integration.

The [fictional cases](../examples/bluesky-publishing.md) are authored expectations,
not live posts or passing behavioural tests. No runner, model/API trial, account
setup, data collection, promotion or publication is required to draft the plan.
