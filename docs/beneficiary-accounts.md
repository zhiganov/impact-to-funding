# Optional accounts from people affected by the work

**Status:** original manual-use guide and proposed session brief, with an
[authored fictional walkthrough](../examples/beneficiary-accounts.md). No live
session, participant trial, privacy configuration or reporting benefit has been
validated. This is not an impact-verification service or a native integration.

Use this when an account of using, trying to use or not using the work could
change a specific decision. People affected by the work supply knowledge and
judgement, not just favourable testimonials. Start with existing permitted
evidence; more collection is not automatically useful.

Ordinary Markdown, a short written account or an appropriate human conversation
can be sufficient. Harmonica is an optional conversation route, not a prerequisite
or a substitute for the intended dedicated ItF project type. No personal theory,
framework, new schema or account is required by ItF's manual route. A chosen
service may require accounts or incur costs; establish its actual requirements.
Offer an accessible non-service route without a new account where available, or
allow no participation. This guide does not change funding.json v1.1.0 financial
fields or make funding contingent on participation.

## 1. Establish the purpose and safe route

Read the [privacy guidance](../PRIVACY.md) before processing real material. Before
inviting anyone, a willing responsible host must establish:

- The decision, relevant period, whose experience is missing and why the burden
  of asking is justified. Include non-use and adverse experiences where relevant;
  an invitation list is not a representative sample.
- The actual recruitment route, expected effort/time limit, accessibility and
  power differences. Participation must not determine access to essential help
  or project benefits. No one owes an account or an explanation for declining.
- Who can see the conversation and use a derivative, including hosts/editors,
  the service and model providers. Establish applicable processing, retention,
  correction and withdrawal arrangements; do not promise anonymity or erasure.
- The permitted purpose/audience, storage outside this repository and a usable
  correction route. Export to another agent/provider, fundraising and publication
  are separate uses, not implied by agreement to a conversation.

If these arrangements cannot be established, use an appropriate alternative or
do not collect. This document does not authorise invitations or data collection.

## 2. Prepare a neutral brief

Adapt the following to the person; it is not a mandatory questionnaire. Replace
the bracketed fields before use. Put claim IDs and the builder's desired verdict
in the private analyst note, not the initial participant conversation.

```text
Purpose: understand experiences of [work's factual function] during [period]
to decide what, if anything, should change.
Context: [minimum neutral facts needed to recognise the work].
Do not assume the person used it, benefited or wants it continued.

Before questions: explain [actual audience and processing], [permitted uses],
[expected effort/time limit], [correction route] and the option to skip or stop.
Check willingness to proceed; do not infer agreement from joining or silence.
Do not request names, contacts or sensitive details just to complete a record.

Invite a concrete occasion of using, trying to use or deciding not to use it.
Explore what happened, what did not work, unwanted effects and what else mattered.
Ask what the person would change, if anything. A comparison with an earlier
situation is optional; uncertainty is valid, not a gap to fill with a guess.
Skip irrelevant or burdensome questions. Do not probe a declined detail.

Offer a short faithful recap and ask what should be corrected or left out.
Keep the person's account separate from facilitator interpretation.
Record recap status: confirmed, corrected or unconfirmed. Silence is not approval.
```

For AI facilitation, inspect the actual generated guide and summary instructions
before use. A prompt is an instruction, not a guarantee of consent behaviour or
neutrality. Do not invent answers, identities or a confirmed recap.

### If using Harmonica

A standalone manually configured session is enough for this optional bridge;
no project, chain, source import or available-template assumption is needed.
The hosted creation contract inspected on 2026-11-07 offers host-only results,
cross-pollination off and widgets off. These are proposed choices here, **not a
tested privacy configuration or complete context-isolation mechanism**.

Before real use, inspect actual result audiences and host/editor access, then
check every context route: host organisational instructions, project inheritance,
attached files/session summaries, retrieval/tools, cross-pollination and links
shared by participants. Do not supply other accounts, expert verdicts or the
builder's desired conclusion to an initial account conversation. Disable
participant-shared links for this route where available: documentation says they
can become session sources for other conversations. Turning off cross-pollination
alone does not address that path. A standalone session can still inherit host
instructions; a project-memory toggle does not establish complete separation.

Check actual account/plan/credit requirements and provider handling. Host-only
results do not mean anonymous, host-blind or confidential. If the required
separation cannot be established, use an appropriate separate route or do not
collect. Do not change shared host settings without authority. Removing a source
does not recall existing conversation context or exports.

Official sources read 2026-11-07: [creating sessions](https://help.harmonica.chat/guides/hosts/creating-sessions.md),
[session settings](https://help.harmonica.chat/guides/hosts/session-settings.md),

These describe product behaviour; no live configuration, deployed enforcement,

## 3. Hand off only permitted material

Use a small private Markdown note, not a raw transcript export. Apply the
[minimum claim record](../references/claims.md#minimum-useful-claim-record):

```text
Receiving claim ID and exact report version:
Safe internal account reference, only if useful:
Observation period, account date and actual source inspected:
Participant statement or permitted faithful paraphrase:
Facilitator recap and confirmation/correction status:
Analyst interpretation, contrary evidence and uncertainty:
Missing perspectives, selection and source-access limits:
Permitted purpose, audience, provider/export use and retention limits:
Responsible correction route, where established:
What this supports / does not support; actual analyst role and assessment date:
```

Do not export identifiers, contacts or sensitive links merely to preserve
provenance. A recap is not the participant's wording unless actually adopted;
an analyst's assessment is not independent review. Inaccessible private evidence
stays uninspected, not disproved or independently verified. If no safe useful
derivative is permitted, leave it restricted and qualify or omit dependent
claims. Public availability is not a requirement for evidence to be meaningful.

## 4. Revise the report and decision

Use the existing [report](../assets/report.md), not a second assessment engine:

- **What happened:** link permitted accounts to exact claims/periods. Distinguish
  an experience, an output and a causal claim; preserve other contributors.
- **Evidence and actual review:** retain conflicting accounts, missingness,
  selection limits and recap status. Nonresponse establishes neither benefit
  nor harm, refusal or approval. Do not average disparate accounts into a score
  or infer population prevalence, time savings or workload reduction.
- **Harms, agency and burden:** include barriers and non-use without pressing
  for vulnerabilities or details the person declined to provide.
- **What changed:** narrow unsupported wording and identify the next useful
  continue/change/support-existing/defer/stop decision. A proposed action is not
  an accepted responsibility or completed improvement.

Preserve the prior report as superseded only where retention is authorised. A
review of an earlier version does not transfer to the revision. Follow the
[correction and use rules](../references/claims.md#updates-reviews-and-disputes)
if an account changes or use permission changes: stop affected uses, reassess
dependent claims and correct controlled derivatives as appropriate. Keep only
permitted history; do not retain prohibited content for an audit trail or
promise deletion of others' copies.

A public report requires separate review of the exact wording, destination and
disclosure permissions. It is not a direct session export. Expert review, mentor
matching and longitudinal project orchestration remain separate optional work;
none is needed to use this guide.
