# Network and systems-method source review

**Date:** 2026-11-25. **Status:** a small optional learning batch delivered one
candidate pattern, P003, with a source-linked method basis. It adds no outcome
case, framework mandate or behavioural claim.

## Scope and evidence

The concrete gap was reasoning rehearsal: the library offered historical cases
and interpretive patterns, but no optional dry run before applying an analogy or
making a causal claim. The source candidates were Converge's Network Toolkit and
The Necessary Tangle. Their source links and remaining research directions are
tracked in [#43](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/43).

The batch uses three exercises in The Necessary Tangle's
[practice pack](https://transduction.systems/systems-thinking/practice/), published
7 September 2026 and inspected 2026-11-25:

- **Transposing theory:** abstract relationships, identify non-correspondence,
  derive a modest intervention and return to the situation.
- **Participative inquiry:** distinguish influence from being affected, audit
  sampling and make participation accessible.
- **Evaluation and reflexivity:** separate measured change from causation, retain
  negative accounts and inspect the evaluator's own choices.

The [P003 note](../../references/library/patterns/rehearse-transfer-and-claim.md)
interprets their shared teaching arrangement. It includes a defective-model or
claim repair and a changed case, without reproducing their scenarios or answers.
The source explicitly says that exercises do not establish professional competence
or real-world practice. Its alpha material has not had independent specialist
pedagogical review.

Converge's [network-health indicators](https://www.converge.net/toolkit/indicators-of-network-health),
[rapid coordination](https://www.converge.net/toolkit/rapid-coordination),
[group agreements](https://www.converge.net/toolkit/group-agreements-for-networks-2)
and [social network analysis](https://www.converge.net/toolkit/conducting-a-social-network-analysis)
were inspected as source candidates. They inform future learning questions about
relationships, autonomy, power and missing people. No new case note was written:
these are methods and illustrative accounts, not an independently inspected
outcome study. The linked participant survey was not read.

The Necessary Tangle's explicit semantics and relational-depth programme were
also reviewed. They separate relationship kinds, source evidence, scope, status
and structural coverage. They support the existing library rules rather than a
new graph service or schema.

## Remaining limits

This is a method-inspired candidate, not proof that rehearsal improves practice.
It does not add Cynefin support, complete the food-system gap, or introduce a
new mandatory workflow. Any future learner exercise or practitioner review
needs its own concrete question, scope and evidence of what actually happened.
