# Initial-user learning plan

**Prepared 2026-11-05. Scope: feedback preparation and readiness assessment only.**
No participants are claimed, no invitation has been sent and no responses have
been collected through this plan. It does not launch recruitment, monitoring,
data collection, model trials or a paid evaluation programme.

## Decision and smallest useful approach

Learn whether the manual skill helps people make an actual decision or prepare
an honest reusable draft with tolerable effort, and where it misleads or fails.
Use one self-chosen small task and optional short feedback, not a mandatory study,
dashboard, certification process or fundraising campaign.

The [participant guide](user-feedback.md) supplies the invocation and questions.
People can use an existing file-reading assistant and keep their own responses
until a collection arrangement is actually agreed. No survey service, installation
repair, new runner, application, MCP service or fresh release is required.

## Collection agreement before recruitment

Use a short plain-language notice, not a new consent platform. Establish:

- The actual willing collection/review owner and existing response channel, who
  can read it, and the model/provider processing involved if responses enter AI.
- The specific learning purpose, fields actually needed, retention period/review
  date and correction/withdrawal route the owner can genuinely support.
- Participation and each answer are voluntary. No effect on essential help or
  any promised funding; do not imply this project operates an eligibility scheme.
- No raw conversations, private packets, contact lists, beneficiary stories,
  donor details, credentials or unnecessary project/participant identities.
- Separate permissions for reviewing a response, later contact and publication
  of a particular quotation/example. Default: no public quotation or recontact.
- Contact details for an opted-in follow-up are separate from learning notes and
  are not public metadata. Do not promise anonymity or erasure beyond actual control.

Do not silently appoint maintainers as support staff, invent dates or choose a
new paid service to fill these unknowns. An existing agreed private conversation
can suffice if its access, use and retention are understood. Actual returned
feedback stays outside the distributable repository and public tracker.

## Reading feedback and deciding what changes

Record only what was actually reported or inspected, its version/task context
and limitations. Use a compact private note; missing answers stay missing.

| Signal | Interpretation and useful response |
|---|---|
| Clearer decision or usable draft | Ask what changed compared with the person's initial intention or usual method; an impression of clarity is not proof of better impact or an independent judgement. |
| Time, questioning and correction burden | Retain rough effort and what caused it; simplify instructions when the burden does not serve the decision. Do not invent an average or impose time tracking. |
| Misleading claim, fabricated reviewer or unsafe disclosure | Distinguish the actual supplied context from unknowns. Stop using the affected output; handle private details through the agreed route. Any public issue needs a separate safe reproduction and disclosure review, not a raw transcript upload. |
| No output, abandonment or refusal to use it | Preserve the reason, including file-access problems and confusing instructions. Do not drop failed attempts or count nonresponse as satisfaction. |
| Fundraising attempt or result | Keep draft/use/ask/rejection/pending/support distinct, with the real period, missingness and other influences. Do not infer that the skill caused donations or social benefit. No fundraising attempt is required. |
| Changed or stopped project direction | Can be a useful decision rather than a conversion failure. Do not pressure someone to continue or fundraise to make the skill look successful. |

Review failures as well as successes before choosing one concrete improvement,
keeping useful feedback that cannot be quantified. Link any resulting change to
the specific finding and affected source version. Existing issues may already
cover it; filing, source edits and public reporting need their applicable scope.

If summarising learning later, state how feedback was obtained, source versions,
actual attempts/responses known, dropouts/nonresponse and unknown denominators,
selection and host differences. Do not manufacture completion rates, representative
results, causal effects or an impact score from a few self-selected accounts.
No learning result is claimed by this preparation document.
