# MTM Together

A reading note from 2026-11-21 on [MTM Together](https://together-community.mtm.now/), and what it means for ItF and [Funding to Impact (FtI)](https://itfti.org/fti).

**Source.** The community's home page, read on 2026-11-21. Figures are its own. Prices aren't shown on the page.

## What it is

MTM Together is a paid online community for nonprofit staff learning to use AI. It's run by [Meet the Moment](https://mtm.now/), a nonprofit technology advisory working on AI, cybersecurity and data privacy. It says it has 300+ members.

Membership includes:
- **Live sessions:** townhalls where members share wins and problems, and member-led Show & Tells where people demo what they've built.
- **Discussion spaces** on tools, workflows and AI policies.
- **AI news with a nonprofit angle,** covering tools, privacy and governance.
- **Resources,** such as data-classification guidance and AI safety checks.

Separate paid "accelerators" run alongside: structured cohorts with a schedule, which include community access. One, "Vibe Coding for Nonprofits", started on 13 November 2026.

## A name to keep straight

The same words name something else. The Trust-Based Philanthropy Project's [Meet the Moment](https://www.trustbasedphilanthropy.org/meet-the-moment) is a pledge for funders, run with GEO and NCFP, and it appears in our [research on trust-based funders](2026-11-21-participatory-and-trust-based-funders.md). Its page links only to the Trust-Based Philanthropy Project's own site, not to mtm.now. They look like separate organisations sharing a name.

## What this suggests for ItF and FtI

These are suggestions, not findings about ItF or FtI:

- **They're the users in ItF's chat-app gap.** Members work in chat apps, not coding harnesses ([#143](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/143)). Their stated concerns are tool choice, donor and client privacy, and AI policies. ItF keeps material local and holds claims to what evidence supports, and both speak to those concerns.
- **Fundraisers are a second audience.** Many members work in fundraising and advancement. ItF's support-brief guidance and its pre-publication claim check fit their work, though ItF is written for builders today.
- **Show & Tells are a place to test ItF with real users,** once it works in a chat app.
- **Some members work at funders.** The page quotes staff from foundations and funder networks. That's a possible listening route for FtI.

Any contact or presentation needs its own approval.
