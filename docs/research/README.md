# Research

Dated research notes for builders and for ItF's guidance. Each note says where its findings came from and when. Check a funder's own page before relying on terms or deadlines.

- [Who pays for maintenance and coordination](2026-11-21-maintenance-and-coordination-funding.md), 2026-11-21: funders of upkeep for open tools and community coordination, the scale of the gap, and how to frame a request.
- [How participatory and trust-based funders decide, and how to reach one](2026-11-21-participatory-and-trust-based-funders.md), 2026-11-21: how funders that devolve decisions or give with light paperwork choose grantees, and routes in for a small builder.
- [What gets a tool taken up by communities](2026-11-21-tool-uptake-in-communities.md), 2026-11-21: peers, training and support after it, open licences, and how to tell whether uptake lasted.
- [How 360Giving won funder adoption](2026-11-21-360giving-adoption.md), 2026-11-21: how a UK open grants-data standard grew to about 355 funders, and where it struggles.
- [What ItF's research means for FtI](2026-11-21-what-this-means-for-fti.md), 2026-11-21: format additions and an adoption approach for Funding to Impact, drawn from the notes above.
- [FEST State of the Field 2026](2026-11-21-fest-state-of-the-field.md), 2026-11-21: a reading of FEST's first report on financing systemic transformation, and what it suggests for ItF and FtI.
- [MTM Together](2026-11-21-mtm-together.md), 2026-11-21: an AI community for nonprofit staff, as a place to test ItF in chat apps and to listen to funders.
