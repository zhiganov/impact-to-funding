## Before distribution claims become stronger

- Confirm the actual distribution channel/version and accurately label tested versus untested hosts. A public source repository is not a completed formal release.
- Keep private evidence excluded from the actual commit/archive, not only from visible examples or `.gitignore` defaults.
- Review exact public text/destination before any community announcement or partnership outreach. No draft authorises sending itself.

No further semantic trial, paid run, integration, formal tag/release or participant recruitment is started by this preparation review. Actual human usefulness, funding outcomes and social benefit remain to be learned through appropriate later use, not asserted from documentation readiness.
