# Try one task and tell us what helped or failed

**Voluntary early feedback; experimental manual use.** These instructions are a
prepared participant guide, not evidence that people have tried it. No recruitment
or response collection is launched by publishing this document.

You can skip any question, stop at any time or decide not to share feedback.
This is not certification, a funding application or a promise of support.

## Before supplying material

- Use an AI assistant you already have that can read local Markdown files. Keep
  the whole source folder, including `references/` and `assets/`, together.
  Ask it to read `SKILL.md`; automatic installation/discovery is not verified.
- Read the [privacy guidance for this source revision](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/blob/87e515a9f9e5bc23193f82e2e6eb4c3781ec8d9b/PRIVACY.md).
  Local files can reach your model provider and remain in its history. Normal
  host costs and terms apply; this guide supplies no account or free model usage.
- Choose a small task and an effort limit that suits you. Public, fictional or
  appropriately authorised material is enough. No donor list, beneficiary story,
  confidential transcript, full personal mission or demonstrated past impact is needed.
- Keep outputs private and outside the distributable source folder. Nothing needs
  to be written to disk: a response in your existing conversation can be enough.

## One concrete invocation

Choose one useful decision: develop a theory, examine a claim, prepare a funding
brief, review progress or connect contributions across projects. Briefly note what
you wanted to decide and what you would otherwise have used; "not sure" is fine.

In the assistant with the source folder available, use this prompt, replacing the
two bracketed parts with your own task and minimal permitted context:

> Read SKILL.md and the relevant supporting files in this source folder, then
> follow the skill. Help me with [one task and the decision it should inform].
> Use only this supplied context: [short, permitted description or records].
> Keep the result private and proportionate. Separate observed facts, my actual
> contribution, shared project results, proposals and unknowns. Do not invent
> evidence, consent, reviewers, dates, budgets or funding links. Do not browse,
> publish, contact anyone, collect new data or change an account.

If you prefer not to use your own material, use only the pinned folder's
`examples/builder-case.md` as the supplied input. Its facts are entirely fictional;
do not reuse them as your own impact claims. The accompanying illustrative answer
is authored guidance, not a benchmark result or independent validation.

If the assistant cannot read the files, stop or keep that access failure as
feedback. Do not spend time installing a server or debugging automatic skill
discovery for this activity. If it produces an unsupported or unsafe statement,
do not reuse that statement in a real report or ask.

## Optional feedback: a few sentences are enough

All fields can be skipped. Do not paste the full input, conversation or output.
Keep even short examples non-sensitive; a lightly disguised real case can still
identify someone.

- **Version and context:** source revision/tag, host/model if known, and task type.
  "Unknown" is acceptable; no account identity or project name is required.
- **Usefulness:** what decision, if any, became clearer? What stayed unresolved?
  A justified choice to change, support existing work or stop counts too.
- **Misleading or unsafe output:** did it invent facts, overstate impact, claim
  another person's credit, imply certification/consent, mishandle restrictions or
  ask for unnecessary sensitive information? A non-sensitive description is enough.
- **Effort:** approximate time, avoidable questions and editing/correction burden,
  if you remember. Was the result worth that effort? No stopwatch or receipt needed.
- **Actual use or failure:** did you use it unchanged, edit it, decline to use it,
  stop early or fail to get an output? Why? "Not useful" is valuable feedback.
- **One improvement:** what would make the next attempt more useful or less burdensome?

### Later fundraising outcomes, only if relevant and separately opted in

You do not need to fundraise to participate. If you later choose to report an
outcome, distinguish: no ask attempted, draft not used, ask/conversation attempted,
pending/no response, declined, support received, or unknown. Multiple states can
apply to different attempts. Note the actual period and other likely influences
where useful; no funder names, donor records or exact amounts are required.

An ask sent is not funding received; funding received is not social impact.
Your account does not establish that the skill caused either. Follow-up contact
requires a separate opt-in through an agreed route; it is not automatic.

## Before returning feedback

Wait for a named collection owner to explain the response channel, who can read
it, its use and retention, and how to request a correction or withdrawal. Until
then, keep responses with you. No confidential or anonymous channel is promised
by this repository. Do not put private feedback into public GitHub issues.

Permission to review feedback is not permission to publish it or quote you. Any
public quotation or identifying example needs separate, exact-content approval.
Feedback does not become MIT-licensed merely because the software is. Deletion
requests cannot guarantee removal from provider logs, backups or others' copies.
