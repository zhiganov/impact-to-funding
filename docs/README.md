# Design context

The [audience and positioning note](audience-and-positioning.md) identifies agent-native
builders as the agreed initial primary audience while preserving broader users,
roles and lifecycle needs. Job loss and difficulty getting hired are one
important job to be done, not a universal audience condition. The intended
value also includes meaningful contribution, verifiable impact, peer/community
connections and potential financial support; these are not claims of existing
matching or funding services. Demand and usefulness remain unvalidated.

[Optional Standard.site publishing and discovery](standard-site-publishing.md)
maps approved public reports to existing publication metadata and tools. It is
design only, not an exporter or publication trial; funding.json stays unchanged
and the skill remains usable through ordinary Markdown/manual reading.

[AI Registry and Vercel Skills CLI distribution references](ai-registry-distribution.md)
compare registry/catalog value with direct Git skill distribution, separately
from skills.sh discovery and impact evidence. Adoption is unselected; no CLI
invocation, registry or native-discovery recovery follows from this capture.

[Voluntary user-feedback guide](user-feedback.md) and
[initial-user learning plan](user-learning-plan.md) prepare one-task manual use,
optional feedback and an explicit artifact/access assessment. The packet pins
post-release source `87e515a`, not the older `v0.1.0` archive. Recruitment, response
collection and exact invitation approval remain separate from preparation.

[Individual theories, shared projects and contribution portfolios](individual-project-contributions.md)
records the agreed conceptual relationship and core manual-use source implementation,
tracked in [#22](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/22)
and the extended [#16](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/16).
The [contribution reference](../references/contributions.md) supplies the operational
guidance. Source instructions and package checks do not establish native activation,
model adherence, a hosted tracker or approval for integrations or live actions;
#16's collaborative workflow design remains separate work.

[The preserved scope snapshot](design-scope-2026-11-04.md) records the proposed product/method boundary at the time it was drafted. Its statements that implementation was unselected and licensing/publication undecided are historical. Current package status is in the [project README](../README.md); original material is now under the [MIT licence](../LICENSE).

The snapshot is retained verbatim. It is not a live task queue, a licence grant, a private evidence export, or approval for publication, integrations or money movement.
