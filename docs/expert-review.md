# Optional claim-specific expert review

**Status:** original manual guide with an [authored fictional walkthrough](../examples/expert-review.md).
No reviewer, available pool, real review, live session, privacy configuration or
reporting benefit has been validated. This is not certification, funding
adjudication, an expert-matching service or a native Harmonica integration.

Use a review only when it could change a specific decision. A narrow claim,
existing permitted evaluation or explicit uncertainty may be sufficient instead.
Ordinary Markdown/document exchange remains valid; Harmonica is optional. No
personal theory, account, Groups deployment, new schema or funding.json v1.1.0
financial-field change is required by this manual route. A chosen host may have
its own account, processing and cost requirements.

Apply the [claim guidance](../references/claims.md), [privacy guidance](../PRIVACY.md)
and [report](../assets/report.md). This guide applies the
[scoped-review design baseline](https://github.com/zhiganov/impact-to-funding/issues/15#issuecomment-6655208883),
not a competing review standard. That baseline's proposed app/group behaviours
are not implemented by these documents.

## 1. Choose the route and question

**Existing evaluation:** establish the actual source, original reviewer,
date/version, reviewed subject and permitted reuse. Record what the evaluator
examined separately from what you inspected. Attribute your reference/summary to
you, not to the original reviewer. Missing claim/evidence versions, competence or
independence stay unknown. Do not invent a request, acceptance, new participation
or endorsement; a public link does not establish scope or unrestricted reuse.

**New solicited review:** identify the exact claim/report and evidence versions,
the decision, and competence needed. Technical inspection, domain knowledge and
evaluation methods are different needs; one person need not cover everything.
Seek a suitable person through a legitimate willing-recipient route, not someone
likely to give a favourable verdict. A public profile does not establish
willingness. No actual pool or available reviewer is assumed.

## 2. Draft a request; do not send the private packet by default

Keep this in the requester's private workspace until actual outreach is authorised.
Only an authorised requester can propose the scope; only the reviewer accepts.

```text
Decision and bounded review question:
Exact claim/report version and evidence versions to be examined:
Competence sought and limits of the requested judgement:
Proposed effort, response window and compensation, or explicitly unknown:
Proposed audience, permitted uses, retention and correction/challenge route:
Minimal evidence packet and custodian; proposed recipient and service/provider:
Acceptance, declared interests and actual evidence permissions: not yet established
```

An initial invitation, if separately authorised, contains only a safe description
of the task, effort and proposed arrangements needed to decide whether to accept.
Do not leak restricted claims, accounts, affiliations or links in a notification.
Do not assume a review is free or available, harvest contacts, repeatedly invite,
silently substitute another reviewer or transfer evidence to a new recipient.

Before access, record relevant authorship/coauthorship (including shared work on
other projects), team/close ties, who selects/funds the reviewer and any
outcome-contingent payment. A mentor can offer useful **partner review** or
recuse; assigning an independent role does not establish independence. A
declaration of “none” or no detected tie is not a sufficient independence basis.
Use the [canonical independence rule](../references/claims.md#separate-claim-type-support-and-review-provenance).

The actual custodian must authorise the exact material, recipient, purpose,
audience, service/model processing and finite retention/use arrangements before
transfer. Earlier agreement to a beneficiary conversation does not cover expert
access or another provider. Offer a minimal permitted derivative, narrower
scope or no access; do not grant project-wide access for one review. Keep real
packets outside this repository. Do not demand sensitive detail to fill a gap.

## 3. Examine the packet without manufacturing a finding

Adapt this private session brief only after actual acceptance and permissions:

```text
Examine [exact claim/report version] against [exact permitted evidence versions]
to inform [decision], within [accepted scope and audience/use limits].
Do not aim to confirm the builder's desired verdict or award verified status.

Ask the reviewer to examine definitions, what the packet actually supports,
contrary/missing evidence, other contributors and plausible explanations.
Keep reported experience, observed outcome and causal attribution distinct.
Mark inaccessible evidence as uninspected; do not treat it as a negative result.
Seek only justified, authorised clarification; stopping or narrowing is valid.

AI may structure questions or draft a recap, not own the substantive judgement.
Ask the reviewer to author or explicitly adopt the exact finding attributed to
them, including its reasoning, limits, date, versions and permitted uses.
An unadopted draft, closed session or silence is not a submitted review.
```

For optional Harmonica facilitation, use a standalone manual session rather than
automatic chains. Apply the [documented setup checks and limits](beneficiary-accounts.md#if-using-harmonica):
inspect actual host/editor/result access, host/project inheritance, attached
sources, participant links and retrieval/tools. Host-only results, cross-pollination
off and widgets off are proposed choices, not reviewer-only access, anonymity or
confidentiality. Do not seed an initial independent assessment with other
reviewers' verdicts or project conclusions by default. Inspect the generated
guide and summary instructions; prompts do not enforce permissions or adoption.
If the agreed boundary cannot be established, use an appropriate document route
or do not transfer. No template availability, CLI parity, live privacy settings
or runtime behaviour was tested here. No chain/notification/credit use is needed
to prepare these documents.

## 4. Record the exact finding and separate statuses

Use the existing claim/report record, not a badge or new attestation schema:

```text
Route: solicited review | existing evaluation reference
Target: exact claim/report and evidence versions, or explicit source/target gaps
Reviewer: actual chosen identity; competence and interests/basis or unknowns
Independence: recorded basis | partner review | unknown
Date/method: actual precision; material AI assistance and limits if any
Examined / not examined: actual sources, versions, access and inspection limits
Finding: support assessment and reasoning within the question actually assessed
Authorship/adoption: reviewer-authored or exact text explicitly adopted;
  otherwise unsubmitted AI/curator draft, not a reviewer finding
Permitted uses/audiences and correction/challenge route:
Review status / evidence-use limits / later claim-support reassessment:
```

For an existing evaluation, preserve its original authorship; your separately
attributed reference does not imply new adoption. Do not require public identity
disclosure or reproduce restricted reviewer/source details. An expert does not
authenticate every account, eliminate selection bias or make a sample representative.
Affected people's accounts retain their meaning and use limits; expert judgement
does not replace their voices.

Use the baseline's explicit labels as dated Markdown notes, not claims of
software enforcement:

- **Request:** Drafted -> Sent only on authorised outreach -> Accepted by the
  reviewer, or Declined / Expired; requester withdrawal is separate. Decline or
  expiry is private, creates no attestation/adverse inference and is not approval.
- **Review:** In progress is not completed. Submitted requires actual authorship
  or exact adoption and the accepted scope/interests for the solicited route.
  Existing evaluation referenced is its own route, not a retrospective request.
- **Version/currentness:** Prior-version, Evidence changed and Corrected retain
  the precise target. R2's finding never becomes a review of R3 automatically.
- **Review withdrawn** concerns the reviewer's own statement. **Evidence
  use/access changed** concerns the custodian's authority and affected uses.
  Neither event by itself proves a claim false; reassess remaining permitted
  support separately. “Not assessed: evidence unavailable” is not a negative
  outcome. A packet may be insufficient for stronger wording without establishing
  that the claimed outcome did not occur.

## 5. Correct or challenge safely; curate any public use separately

The reviewer corrects or withdraws their own finding; a report author cannot
rewrite it into endorsement. For evidence-use changes, stop affected uses while
checking the actual basis, then qualify/omit dependent claims as needed. Keep
only authorised history and correct controlled derivatives where permitted;
do not promise recall of conversations, provider logs or exports.

A challenge needs a safe route and an exact target/kind/remedy. Keep the challenge
and protected identity in a separate private note limited to the authorised
handler, not the review packet or shared index. A non-conflicted handler other
than the original reviewer may coordinate a bounded response under the baseline;
none is appointed here. If no
safe route/handler exists or agreed effort is exhausted, leave it unresolved.
Only safe permitted views show “challenged, unresolved”; qualify/omit dependent
public claims without publishing allegations or exposing the raiser, even by
revealing the challenge's existence. Silence or elapsed time settles nothing.
Do not invent an appeals service or automatic further review.

Public quotation, fundraising and publication need separate review of exact
text, destination, permissions and material qualifications. A session export is
not a cleared public report. This guide authorises no contact, evidence transfer,
real review, funding decision or resource creation; mentorship/matching and
longitudinal project orchestration remain separate scopes.
